## How to Apply

Verification is reviewed manually. To start, email us at [hi@s.ee](mailto:hi@s.ee) from the address associated with your S.EE account and tell us:

- The bio page you want verified (e.g. `s.ee/you`)
- Whether you are applying for **individual** (blue) or **organization** (gold) verification

We will reply by email with the exact documents and information required for your case, then review your submission and let you know the result.

---

## Requirements

### Individual Verification (Blue Check)

For a blue check, you generally need to prove that the bio page represents a real, authentic person. We typically ask for:

- At least **two** social media accounts connected to your bio page through [OAuth](/docs/link-in-bio#social-media) (for example, X, GitHub, and YouTube)
- A meaningful number of followers on at least one of those connected accounts
- Evidence that the connected accounts belong to you

### Organization Verification (Gold Check)

For a gold check, you need to prove that the bio page represents a real, registered organization. We typically ask for:

- Documents that confirm the organization is legally registered (such as a business registration or equivalent)
- Proof that you are authorized to represent the organization
- A connection between the organization and the bio page (for example, an official website or domain)

> **Note:** The exact requirements can vary by case. The list above is a guide — we will confirm what is needed for your application by email.

---

## After Verification

Once approved, the badge appears automatically next to the name on your bio page. If your account or organization details change significantly, contact us at [hi@s.ee](mailto:hi@s.ee) so we can keep your verification up to date.
