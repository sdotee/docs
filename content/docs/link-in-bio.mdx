## Overview

A bio page is a personal landing page, similar to Linktree, that puts all your links and social profiles behind one short URL. Perfect for social media bios and anywhere you only get one link.

For a live example, see our official bio page at [s.ee/see](https://s.ee/see).

---

## Create a Bio Page

1. Log in to [https://s.ee/user/dashboard](https://s.ee/user/dashboard)
2. Click **Link Management > Bio Pages** in the left sidebar or go directly to [https://s.ee/user/bio/](https://s.ee/user/bio/)
3. Click **Create Bio Page**
4. Fill in the details:
   - **Title** - The page title shown in the browser tab
   - **Domain** - Select the domain for your bio page
   - **Alias** - Your handle, which becomes part of the URL (e.g. `s.ee/you`)
5. Click **Create**

> **Important:** The **Domain** and **Alias** cannot be changed after creation. To use a different domain or alias, delete the bio page and create a new one.

Once created, click **... > Edit** on your bio page to customize it.

## Page Settings

### Basics

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Title | The page title shown in the browser tab |
| Full Name | The name shown on the page. This can be your own name or an organization name |
| Avatar | Upload a custom avatar. If you don't set one, your [Gravatar](https://gravatar.com) is used |
| Description | A short introduction shown on the page |
| Email | Display your email address with an icon on the page |

### Appearance

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Layout Style | Choose between **List** and **Grid** layouts |
| Page theme | Choose a built-in theme. Available themes depend on the selected layout |
| Color mode | Choose **Light** or **Dark**. Some dark themes only have one color scheme and look the same in either mode |
| Background Image | Upload a custom background image. On some themes the background is fixed and cannot be changed |
| Background Color | Set a custom background color. This conflicts with a custom background image, and on some themes the background color is fixed |

### Content

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| RSS Feed | Show a link to your RSS/Atom feed, with an option to display its latest entries (up to 5) |
| Mastodon Profile | Show a link to your Mastodon profile, with an option to display your latest posts (up to 5) |
| Custom Links | Add up to 10 links, each with an optional icon and description. You can choose whether icons and descriptions are shown |

## Social Media

Connect social accounts with OAuth to show them on your bio page. Connected accounts appear as icons below your avatar.

| Platform | Latest Content (Grid layout only) |
|----------|-----------------------------------|
| X | Shows your latest posts |
| YouTube | Shows your latest videos |
| GitHub | Shows repositories of your choice |

> **Note:** Latest content from connected accounts is only displayed in the **Grid** layout. In the **List** layout, only the profile icons are shown.

## Verification

You can apply for a verified badge on your bio page — a blue check for individuals or a gold check for organizations. See [Bio Page Verification](/docs/link-in-bio-verification) for details and how to apply.

## Custom Domains

You can host your bio page on your own [custom domain](/docs/custom-domains) in two ways:

- **Root domain** - Serve the bio page directly at your domain root (e.g. `example.com`) by enabling **Set as the bio page for the root domain**
- **Path-based** - Serve the bio page at a path on your domain (e.g. `example.com/you`)

## Managing Bio Pages

- **Edit** - Click **... > Edit** to change any setting except the domain and alias
- **Archive** - Temporarily hide your bio page from visitors until you activate it again
- **Delete** - Remove the bio page permanently and free up its alias for a new page
