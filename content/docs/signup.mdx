S.EE has no separate sign-up form. Your account is created when you buy a plan, using the email address you enter at checkout.

---

## Buy a Plan

1. Go to the [pricing page](https://s.ee/pricing/) and choose a plan
2. Enter your email address in **Email address**. We'll email your login link here, so make sure it's correct.
3. Choose a payment method and pay

When the payment goes through, your account is created and the plan is active right away.

If you already have an S.EE account, [log in](https://s.ee/user/login/) first and buy from your dashboard. Checking out as a new customer with an email that's already registered fails with "This email is already registered."

---

## Log In with the Email Link

1. Check your inbox for an email titled **[S.EE] Your account is ready**. If you can't find it, check your spam folder.
2. Click **Log in to S.EE** in the email. You're signed in to the dashboard without a password.

The link expires in 24 hours. If it has expired, go to the [login page](https://s.ee/user/login/), choose **Continue with Magic Link**, enter your email, and click **Send Magic Link** to get a new one.

---

## Set a Password and Username

The first time you log in, a **Set a password** window opens:

- **Password** (required): set a password so you can sign in with your email and password next time. The window stays open until a password is set.
- **Username (optional)**: used to sign in and as the default address of your [Bio page](/docs/link-in-bio/). A username can only be set once. Click **Skip for now** to leave it, and set it later in Settings.

Click **Save and continue** to finish.

---

## Team Invitations

If someone invited you to their team, open the link in the invitation email to create your account.
